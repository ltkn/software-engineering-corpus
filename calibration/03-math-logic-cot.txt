Q: Prove S(n): 1 + 2 + ... + n = n(n+1)/2 for all natural n.
Step 1. Base: n=1 gives 1 = 1(2)/2. Holds.
Step 2. Assume S(k). Then 1+...+k+(k+1) = k(k+1)/2 + (k+1) = (k+1)(k+2)/2. That is S(k+1).
Step 3. By induction S(n) holds for all n. The step must derive S(k+1) from S(k) alone; assuming the conclusion for k+1 would be circular.
Answer: Proved by induction with explicit base and step.

Q: A test has 99% sensitivity and 99% specificity for a defect present in 1% of checkouts. A checkout tests positive. Probability it is truly defective?

Step 3. P(defect|pos) = 0.99(0.01)/0.0198 = 0.5.
Answer: 50%. Ignoring the base rate turns a good test into a false alarm machine.

Q: Fibonacci via naive recursion vs iterative DP.

Concept: Order types and when each executes.
Micro-example: stock at 100.00; limit buy at 99.50 fills only at or below 99.50; market buy fills immediately near 100.00 plus spread and impact; stop 98.00 triggers a market sell once touched.
Assumption: quotes shown are tradable size at that instant; thin books slip past them.
Misuse cost: market orders in illiquid opens paying 2% spread as "convenience".
Verification: recompute fill vs quote snapshot; slippage log per order type reviewed.

Goal: Baseline on Python 3.14 (current stable as of 2026-10; 3.10 EOL, 3.15 pre-release).
Wiring: requires-python = ">=3.14" in pyproject, CI matrix on 3.13 plus 3.14, deprecation warnings as errors in tests.
Why: 3.10 is end-of-life (no security updates); the floor must be a supported series, and the ceiling the current stable.
Pitfall: requires-python >=3.9 "for compatibility", testing only on 3.14, shipping syntax that breaks on 3.9.
Verification: CI fails on EOL interpreters; version matrix green on the two supported series.

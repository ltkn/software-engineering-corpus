Goal: systemd units with hardening for service processes.
Wiring:

ExecStart=/opt/users-svc/bin/app
Restart=on-failure

Why: hardening flags shrink what a compromised process can touch; restart policy survives crashes without masking them.

Verification: systemd-analyze security score reviewed per unit; restart drill (kill -9) recovers within the SLO.
